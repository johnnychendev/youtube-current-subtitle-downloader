## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-27

### Added

- Initial public release.
- Download of the subtitle track currently displayed by YouTube.
- Manual, automatically generated, and reliably identifiable translated caption support.
- Timestamped UTF-8 TXT output with document metadata.
- Native-style player button with enabled, disabled, loading, and error states.
- Event-driven handling for CC changes, language changes, and YouTube SPA navigation.
- Privacy-focused, dependency-free implementation with no analytics or tracking.
